About the artist

Steph Fontenot

Steph Fontenot is a visual artist based on the Gulf Coast, known for her expressive use of color, texture, and atmosphere. Her work spans acrylics and mixed media, often blending natural landscapes with elements of nostalgia, introspection, and surreal detail.

Her portfolio ranges from impressionistic seascapes and moody abstracts to character-driven pieces and symbolic compositions. Each work carries a narrative thread, capturing the feeling of a place, a memory, or a fleeting moment in time.

Whether for personal spaces, curated collections, or collaborative projects, her pieces offer a thoughtful blend of storytelling and style.
